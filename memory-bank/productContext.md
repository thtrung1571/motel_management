# Product Context

Why this exists:
- Streamline front-desk workflows for small to medium motels.
- Reduce manual errors in pricing and room assignment.
- Provide visibility into occupancy, income, and operational metrics.

Primary users:
- Reception/front-desk staff
- Managers/owners (administration)

Key workflows (happy paths):
- Authenticate and access a dashboard with occupancy and revenue insights.
- Browse room status; create a rental (assign customer, room, expected checkout).
- During stay: add consumables (e.g., drinks) and other charges.
- Checkout: apply pricing rules and finalize invoice.
- Manage rooms, room types, pricing logic, customers, and shifts.

UX goals:
- Fast, low-friction interactions for check-in/checkout.
- Clear at-a-glance room/rental status.
- Consistent, predictable pricing presentation.
